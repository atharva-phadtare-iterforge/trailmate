# Rocky Point Trail

*An exposed coastal trail along dramatic cliffs, with real fall-line exposure in places.*

## Overview

Rocky Point Trail follows a rugged stretch of coastline along the tops of sea cliffs, offering dramatic ocean views but also some of the most exposed trail sections in the area. Wind and weather play a bigger role here than on the inland trails.

## Details

- Difficulty: Hard
- Distance: 8.2 km
- Elevation Gain: 540 m
- Best For: Coastal views, dramatic scenery, experienced hikers comfortable with exposure
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash (exposed sections — use caution)

## Trail Notes

Several sections of trail run close to the cliff edge with a real fall-line drop on one side. The path itself is well-defined and not technically difficult underfoot, but the exposure combined with frequently strong coastal wind makes this a trail to treat with real respect, particularly for anyone uneasy with heights.

Weather changes quickly along this stretch of coast — a clear morning can turn foggy or windy within an hour. Checking a marine forecast, not just a general one, is worth doing before setting out.
