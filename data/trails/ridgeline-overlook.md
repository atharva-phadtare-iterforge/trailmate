# Ridgeline Overlook

*A moderate climb to a wide, open ridge with panoramic views.*

## Overview

Ridgeline Overlook trades shade for scenery — most of the trail is exposed, climbing steadily through open grassland to a ridge that offers a 270-degree view of the valley below. It's a popular sunrise hike, since the ridge faces east and the light is best in the first hour after dawn.

## Details

- Difficulty: Moderate
- Distance: 6.5 km
- Elevation Gain: 420 m
- Best For: Sunrise views, panoramic photography
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash

## Trail Notes

The climb is steady rather than steep, gaining most of its elevation over the middle third of the trail via a series of long switchbacks. There's very little tree cover, so sun exposure and wind are the main things to plan around — bring more water than you think you'll need, especially in summer.

The ridge itself is broad and open, with several informal spots to sit and take in the view. It's a legitimately good sunrise or sunset destination, though a headlamp is worth carrying if you're timing it for first light.
