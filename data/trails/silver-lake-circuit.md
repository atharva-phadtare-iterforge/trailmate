# Silver Lake Circuit

*An easy loop around a clear alpine-fed lake, with a popular swimming spot.*

## Overview

Silver Lake Circuit is a flat loop trail that circles the lake entirely, passing through a mix of open shoreline and light forest. The lake itself is fed by snowmelt and stays cold even in summer, but a sheltered cove about a third of the way around is a popular spot to swim on hot afternoons.

## Details

- Difficulty: Easy
- Distance: 4.0 km
- Elevation Gain: 40 m
- Best For: Swimming, lake views, an easy full loop
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash (off-leash not permitted near the swimming cove)

## Trail Notes

The trail hugs the shoreline closely enough that the lake is visible for nearly the entire loop, which makes it feel shorter and more varied than the distance alone suggests. The swimming cove has a small gravel beach and is shallow enough near the shore to wade in gradually rather than jumping straight into cold water.

Afternoons can get busy in summer, particularly around the cove, but the far side of the lake tends to stay quiet even on crowded days.
