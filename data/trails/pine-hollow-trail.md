# Pine Hollow Trail

*A shaded forest walk through mature pine, moderate but unhurried.*

## Overview

Pine Hollow Trail winds through a mature pine forest with a gentle, rolling elevation profile — never flat, never steep. It's a good option on hot days thanks to near-constant canopy cover, and the forest floor here is unusually open, giving it a distinct, spacious feel compared to denser trails nearby.

## Details

- Difficulty: Moderate
- Distance: 5.4 km
- Elevation Gain: 260 m
- Best For: Shaded hiking, hot-weather outings, a quieter forest walk
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash

## Trail Notes

The trail surface is soft packed dirt covered in pine needles for most of its length, which makes for pleasant footing but can be slippery on the steeper rolling sections after rain. There are no major viewpoints along the way — this is a trail about the walk itself rather than a destination at the end.

Wildlife sightings are relatively common here, particularly deer in the early morning. It stays noticeably cooler than the more exposed trails in the area, making it a solid backup plan on days when the forecast is hotter than expected.
