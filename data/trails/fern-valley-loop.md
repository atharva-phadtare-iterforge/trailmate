# Fern Valley Loop

*A shaded, moderate loop through dense fern understory with one creek crossing.*

## Overview

Fern Valley Loop winds through a cool, moist forest known for its unusually dense fern understory, crossing a small creek partway around before looping back through a different section of forest on the return leg.

## Details

- Difficulty: Moderate
- Distance: 6.0 km
- Elevation Gain: 310 m
- Best For: Shaded hiking, forest scenery, a single manageable creek crossing
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash

## Trail Notes

The forest here is noticeably denser and moister than Pine Hollow Trail, with ferns covering most of the forest floor for long stretches. It stays cool even in summer and can feel almost humid after rain.

The creek crossing, roughly at the trail's midpoint, is normally an easy rock-hop and rarely requires wading except after unusually heavy rain. The return leg climbs gently through a different part of the forest, giving the loop more variety than an out-and-back covering the same distance would.
