# Old Mill Loop

*A flat, easy loop past the ruins of a 19th-century mill, dog-friendly throughout.*

## Overview

Old Mill Loop is a flat, easy walk that circles past the stone ruins of a 19th-century mill before returning along the millpond's edge. It combines a bit of local history with an easy, dog-friendly outing.

## Details

- Difficulty: Easy
- Distance: 2.5 km
- Elevation Gain: 20 m
- Best For: Dogs, casual walks, a bit of local history
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash

## Trail Notes

The trail surface is flat and well-maintained the entire way, making it accessible for strollers as well as dogs. The mill ruins themselves are fenced off but visible and include a small interpretive sign explaining the site's history.

The millpond section on the return leg is a pleasant, quiet stretch, generally the least crowded part of the loop even when the trailhead parking lot is full.
