# Willow Creek Wander

*A flat, easy stroll alongside Willow Creek.*

## Overview

Willow Creek Wander is about as low-key as trails get — a flat, wide path that follows the creek through open meadow. It's a favorite for an easy morning walk, a first hike with a dog, or anyone who wants to be outside without any real physical demand.

## Details

- Difficulty: Easy
- Distance: 2.1 km
- Elevation Gain: 15 m
- Best For: Dogs, casual walks, beginners
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, off-leash in the designated meadow section

## Trail Notes

The entire trail is flat and wide enough for two people to walk side by side comfortably. There's a fenced meadow section about halfway along where dogs are allowed off-leash, which makes this one of the more popular trails with dog owners in the area.

There's not much in the way of scenery beyond the creek itself and open meadow, but that's more or less the point — it's a reliable, easy trail for when you want time outside without planning around it.
