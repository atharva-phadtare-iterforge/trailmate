# Blackrock Summit

*A demanding scramble to the highest point in the area.*

## Overview

Blackrock Summit is the hardest maintained trail in the region — a long approach through forest followed by an exposed rock scramble to the summit. The reward is a genuinely unique 360-degree view, but this is not a casual outing and shouldn't be treated as one.

## Details

- Difficulty: Hard
- Distance: 11.0 km
- Elevation Gain: 980 m
- Best For: Experienced hikers, summit views, a real physical challenge
- Dogs Allowed: Not recommended (exposed scrambling)

## Trail Notes

The first 7 kilometers are a fairly standard, if steep, forest climb. The final stretch is where the trail earns its difficulty rating: a rock scramble with real exposure on both sides, requiring hands-on-rock movement in a few sections. It is not technical climbing, but it is not something to attempt in wet or icy conditions.

Start early. The round trip regularly takes 6-8 hours, and the exposed summit scramble is best avoided in afternoon thunderstorm season. Sturdy boots with real grip are non-negotiable here.
