# Sunset Bluff Trail

*A moderate climb timed well for evening views over the valley.*

## Overview

Sunset Bluff Trail climbs to a west-facing bluff that has become one of the most popular evening destinations in the area, offering a clear, unobstructed view over the valley as the sun goes down.

## Details

- Difficulty: Moderate
- Distance: 5.5 km
- Elevation Gain: 380 m
- Best For: Sunset views, evening hikes, photography
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash

## Trail Notes

The climb is steady throughout with no single especially steep section, making the pacing fairly predictable. Most people time their start so they reach the bluff 30-45 minutes before sunset, which means the trail (and the bluff itself) can get genuinely crowded on clear evenings.

A headlamp is strongly recommended for the way down, since the descent in fading light is where most trail incidents here happen — the trail itself is safe, but the light doesn't last as long as people expect.
