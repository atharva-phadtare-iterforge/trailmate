# Cascade Falls Loop

*A short, family-friendly loop to a two-tier waterfall.*

## Overview

Cascade Falls Loop is a gentle, well-shaded walk that follows Cascade Creek up to a two-tier waterfall before looping back through a stand of old-growth cedar. It's one of the most popular short hikes in the area, especially with families and anyone looking for a quick, satisfying payoff without much climbing.

## Details

- Difficulty: Easy
- Distance: 3.2 km
- Elevation Gain: 90 m
- Best For: Families, waterfall views, short outings
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash

## Trail Notes

The trail is wide and well-maintained for its entire length, with a mix of packed dirt and boardwalk sections over the wetter areas near the creek. The waterfall viewpoint has a small platform that can get crowded on weekend afternoons, so an early start is worth it if you want the falls to yourself.

There's no real elevation to speak of — the "loop" part gains just enough height to give a slightly different angle on the falls from the return leg. Good footwear is still recommended since the boardwalk sections can be slick when wet.
