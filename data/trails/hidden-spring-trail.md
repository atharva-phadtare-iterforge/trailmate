# Hidden Spring Trail

*A short, shaded walk to a small natural spring, easy but genuinely pretty.*

## Overview

Hidden Spring Trail is a short, mostly flat walk through shaded forest to a small natural spring that feeds a clear, shallow pool. It's less well-known than some of the other easy trails in the area, which means it tends to stay quiet even on busy weekends.

## Details

- Difficulty: Easy
- Distance: 3.0 km
- Elevation Gain: 60 m
- Best For: A quiet easy walk, a small water feature, avoiding crowds
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash

## Trail Notes

The trail stays shaded for almost its entire length, following a gentle, mostly flat grade with only a couple of short, easy rises near the spring itself. The spring pool is small — more of a clear, still pocket of water than a swimming spot — but it's a genuinely peaceful place to sit for a while before heading back.

Because it's less heavily trafficked than Silver Lake or Cascade Falls, this is a good pick for anyone specifically looking to avoid crowds on an easy trail.
