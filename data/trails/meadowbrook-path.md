# Meadowbrook Path

*A short, flat wildflower walk, ideal for young kids.*

## Overview

Meadowbrook Path is a short interpretive trail through open wildflower meadow, popular with families and school groups in spring and early summer when the flowers are at their peak.

## Details

- Difficulty: Easy
- Distance: 1.8 km
- Elevation Gain: 10 m
- Best For: Young children, wildflowers, short outings
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash

## Trail Notes

The whole path is flat, wide, and shaded only at the very start — bring sun protection. Best visited in late spring when the meadow is in full bloom.
