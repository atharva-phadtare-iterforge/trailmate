# Eagle's Perch Trail

*A steep, switchbacking climb to an exposed viewpoint favored by raptors.*

## Overview

Eagle's Perch Trail climbs almost continuously via tight switchbacks to a narrow rock outcrop that offers one of the best long-distance views in the area. The outcrop is a known perching spot for local raptors, which is where the trail gets its name — sightings are common, though not guaranteed.

## Details

- Difficulty: Hard
- Distance: 9.8 km
- Elevation Gain: 760 m
- Best For: Experienced hikers, birdwatching, long-distance views
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash (exposed final approach — use caution)

## Trail Notes

The switchbacks are relentless rather than technical — there's no scrambling involved, but the sustained grade makes this a genuine cardio test, particularly in the middle third where the trail gains most of its elevation. Trekking poles are worth bringing.

The final approach to the outcrop narrows and has real exposure on one side, though the path itself remains a clear, walkable trail rather than a scramble. Worth timing for mid-morning, when thermal air currents bring raptors close to the outcrop most reliably.
