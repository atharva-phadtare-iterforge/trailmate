# Timberline Pass

*A long alpine route above the treeline, with real potential for snow even in summer.*

## Overview

Timberline Pass climbs above the treeline into genuine alpine terrain, crossing a high pass that can hold patches of snow well into summer in some years. It's a long, demanding day out that rewards hikers with terrain unlike anything else in the area.

## Details

- Difficulty: Hard
- Distance: 14.0 km
- Elevation Gain: 1050 m
- Best For: Alpine scenery, experienced hikers, a serious full-day objective
- Dogs Allowed: Not recommended (alpine terrain, possible snow)

## Trail Notes

The lower two-thirds of the trail climb steadily through progressively thinning forest before breaking above the treeline entirely for the final approach to the pass. Above the treeline, the trail becomes rockier and less distinct in places, and route-finding requires more attention than on any other trail covered here.

Snow can persist in shaded gullies near the pass well into July in higher-snowfall years, and an ice axe or microspikes may be worth carrying early in the season. This is the most weather-dependent trail in the area — conditions should be checked close to the actual hiking date, not days in advance.
