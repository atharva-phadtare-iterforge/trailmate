# Cascade Falls Extension

*A longer variation of the Cascade Falls Loop, continuing past the falls to a second viewpoint.*

## Overview

The Cascade Falls Extension picks up where the standard Cascade Falls Loop turns back, continuing another kilometer upstream to a quieter second viewpoint above the falls. It keeps the same easy, family-friendly character as the shorter loop but adds a bit more distance and a gentle climb near the end.

## Details

- Difficulty: Easy-Moderate
- Distance: 5.5 km
- Elevation Gain: 180 m
- Best For: Waterfall views without the crowds, slightly longer outings
- Dogs Allowed: Yes, on leash

## Trail Notes

Everything about the first two kilometers matches the standard Cascade Falls Loop — same creekside path, same boardwalk sections, same first waterfall viewpoint. Past that point, the crowds thin out noticeably as the trail narrows and begins a gradual climb along the canyon wall.

The upper viewpoint looks down on the falls from above rather than head-on, which is a genuinely different experience from the main loop, not just more of the same. Worth doing if you've already done the short loop before and want a new angle on a familiar trail.
